# Missing ownership turns engineering work into waiting cost

Missing ownership is one of the quiet ways engineering systems create waste.

When nobody owns the next decision, work does not disappear. It waits in review, waits in planning, waits in handoff, or waits for someone else to clarify the next move.

TeamStation AI published the full note here:

https://teamstation.dev/research/articles/missing-ownership-is-distributed-engineering-cost

The operational check is simple: name the decision owner before adding more people to the work.
