# Managerial Directive as engineering operating evidence

Date: 2026-07-30
Source: https://engineering.teamstation.dev/teams/managerial-directive/

## Engineering Note

Every extra agent turns management intent into executable system behavior.

A vague ticket used to waste one engineer's afternoon. In an agentic workflow, the same ambiguity can launch parallel code changes, tests, documents, and tool calls before anyone discovers the starting assumption was wrong. Direction now has a blast radius.

The Managerial Directive doctrine puts the CTO inside the system graph. The work is to define the objective, bound the tools, assign approval points, and keep human judgment at decisions where architecture and production risk meet. Automated tests can verify behavior. They cannot decide whether the team solved the right problem.

Put that workflow across LATAM time zones and vague direction becomes visible fast. Context has to survive async handoffs without private translation. One objective, explicit constraints, named approvals, a stopping condition, and an owner give both engineers and agents a path they can execute and explain.
