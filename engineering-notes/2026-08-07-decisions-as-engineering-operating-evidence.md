## Engineering Note

A polished resume used to cost real time. Now AI can make one in minutes, so two engineers can read as identical on paper while the thinking underneath is miles apart. Same wrapper, very different engine.

That changes the hiring problem. Keywords are labels, not proof, bc `Java AND AWS` can find words but it cannot show how a person breaks down a hard system, changes direction when an idea fails, or explains a decision under pressure.

Inside TeamStation's distributed engineering operating system, we treat that gap like signal processing. The Decisions doctrine lays out the public model: isolate small evaluation units, map meaning in vector space, measure the traits hidden under the words, then choose the next question by how much uncertainty it can remove. In basic English, stop rewarding the best wrapper and test the thinking inside it.

For distributed AI engineering teams in LATAM, geography comes after the signal. The full Decisions pillar shows the math and evaluation logic behind that order:
