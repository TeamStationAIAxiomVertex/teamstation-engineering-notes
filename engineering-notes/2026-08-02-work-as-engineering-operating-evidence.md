## Engineering Note

A busy engineering team can still be a traffic jam. More tickets enter, AI creates more code, review stays finite, and wait time starts eating the delivery plan.

Queue math explains the mess. When arrival rate gets close to available capacity, small surprises create long delays. Old branches become inventory, context decays, and ppl start new work because finishing work is harder.

Our Work doctrine puts queueing kinetics, Little's Law, code inventory, cost of delay, and WIP control on one map. I picked the pillar today bc it shows how those parts connect before we go deeper into each one.

https://engineering.teamstation.dev/work/

Distributed LATAM delivery is where the model gets tested. Clean limits, visible queues, daily integration, and deployed proof keep async work from turning into silent backlog.
