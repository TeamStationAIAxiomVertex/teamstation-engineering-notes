Date: 2026-08-05

## Engineering Note

Cost of delay is where engineering stops being a task list and becomes business math.

A late decision, a blocked review, or a weak dependency is not just inconvenience. It is lost option value, lost learning, and sometimes lost revenue.

TeamStation uses the page to connect delivery behavior to economic consequence. For CTOs and CIOs, the page matters because AI speed means little if delay keeps hiding inside decisions and handoffs.

Distributed LATAM delivery benefits when delay is visible, because distance stops being an excuse and becomes a measurable operating variable.
