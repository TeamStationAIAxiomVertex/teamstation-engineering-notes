## Engineering Note

# Review latency is paid engineering time waiting for a decision

Review latency should be treated as an engineering capacity signal, not only a process complaint.

A pull request that waits has not become accepted work yet. The useful measurement is the time between submission, first meaningful review, decision, and accepted or released evidence.

That reading gets stronger when it is joined to blocker age, change size, rework, ownership, and final acceptance.
