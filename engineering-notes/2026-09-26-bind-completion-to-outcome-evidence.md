## Engineering note: bind completion to outcome evidence

A closed work item records a workflow transition. The weird part is that it doesn't show whether the intended result exists or whether unresolved risk still changes the next decision for the CTO.

For material work, capture the intended outcome, acceptance boundary, delivered artifact or behavior, production evidence, remaining risk, and decision owner. Keep ticket state as one signal inside that record.

This evidence model fits beside [release readiness ownership](https://teamstation.dev/research/articles/release-readiness-owner-evidence) and [engineering rework measurement](https://teamstation.dev/research/articles/measure-engineering-rework-before-fire). Together they separate completed activity from verified delivery value.
