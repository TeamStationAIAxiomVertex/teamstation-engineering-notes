# Engineering Telemetry

Engineering telemetry turns delivery work into observable signals. It helps CTOs and CIOs see review cost, repair cost, Time to Isolation, handoff risk, test gaps, and hidden rework.

- What signals predict AI engineering team performance?

- How does telemetry show delivery risk before a deadline slips?
